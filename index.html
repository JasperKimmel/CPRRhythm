Hands-Free on Apple Watch

Precise haptic feedback keeps you locked in at 110 compressions per minute — the recommended CPR tempo.

Breathing Alerts

Automatic reminders after every 30 compressions to give rescue breaths, following official guidelines.

Fully localised in English and Dutch so you can use the app confidently in your own language.
