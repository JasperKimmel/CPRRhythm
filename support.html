Frequently Asked Questions

Which devices does CPR Rhythm support?

CPR Rhythm is designed for Apple Watch. The companion iPhone app is required for installation. It works with Apple Watch Series 4 and later running watchOS 7 or higher.

Why 110 compressions per minute?

The European Resuscitation Council (ERC) and American Heart Association (AHA) recommend a compression rate of 100–120 per minute. CPR Rhythm uses 110 BPM as the midpoint of this range.

Do I need to keep my iPhone nearby while using the app?

No. Once the app is started on your Apple Watch, it runs independently. You do not need your iPhone nearby.

Does the app collect any personal or health data?

No. CPR Rhythm does not collect, store, or transmit any personal or health data. Only your in-app settings are saved locally on your device. See our privacy policy for full details.

Can CPR Rhythm replace a CPR course?

No. CPR Rhythm is an assistive tool only. It is strongly recommended that all users have completed a basic CPR training course. The app does not replace formal training or professional medical guidance.

The haptic feedback is not working. What can I do?

Make sure that haptic alerts are enabled on your Apple Watch. Go to the Watch app on your iPhone → Sounds & Haptics, and ensure Haptic Strength is turned up and that Silent Mode is not preventing haptics. Also check that Do Not Disturb is not enabled during use.
