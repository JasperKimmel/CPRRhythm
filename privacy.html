Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 18th 2026

CPR Rhythm does not collect, share, or sell any personal data. The only data stored by the app is your personal settings, which remain entirely on your device.

Who we are

CPR Rhythm is an Apple Watch application developed by Jasper Kimmel. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data we collect

CPR Rhythm collects no personal data. The app may write a minimal workout entry to the Apple Health app if you grant permission. No Health data is read by the app, and no data leaves your device.

The only data the app stores are your in-app settings and preferences (such as language preferences). This data is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to any server or third party.

Third-party services

CCPR Rhythm does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking. Health data remains on your device and is managed by Apple’s Health app.

Health data (HealthKit)

CPR Rhythm uses Apple’s HealthKit framework to enable a long-running workout session during CPR guidance. This allows the app to remain active and responsive on Apple Watch while you use it.

What we request: The app requests permission to write the workout type only (e.g., to save a minimal workout entry). The app does not request permission to read your Health data.

What we collect or store: CPR Rhythm does not collect, transmit, or sell any Health data. If you allow it, the app may save a minimal workout entry to the Health app to support the session. No Health data leaves your device.

Control and revocation: You can grant or revoke Health permissions at any time in the iOS Health app (Settings → Privacy & Security → Health, or in the Health app under your profile → Apps). If you revoke permissions, CPR Rhythm continues to function, but long-running session behavior may be limited.

Apple's data practices

CPR Rhythm is distributed via the Apple App Store and TestFlight. Apple may collect certain usage and diagnostic data in accordance with their own privacy policy. This data is handled entirely by Apple and is not accessible to the developer. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy at apple.com/privacy for more information.

Children's privacy

CPR Rhythm does not knowingly collect any data from children. As the app collects no personal data at all, it is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy changes in the future, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us via the support page.
